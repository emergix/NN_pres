# General Presentation of AI centred around the neural network technology.
This document presents a comprehensive strategy and conceptual exploration around Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) applied in a financial context. It discusses the philosophical, technical, and strategic directions for integrating AGI technologies within Natixis' operations and market positioning.

Key topics covered include:
- The vision of AGI as a synthesis of learning, information theory, and physical law.
- Practical applications in trading, data modeling, and financial intelligence.
- The importance of modular design and information-theoretic foundations.
- Strategic roadmap for AGI initiatives.
